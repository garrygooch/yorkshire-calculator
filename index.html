Air Fryer — Fresh or Thawed Batter

1. Add 1 tsp oil to ramekin; preheat at 200°C for 4–5 mins until smoking hot.

2. Whisk batter briefly, pour into sizzling oil, bake undisturbed at 200°C for 10–12 mins.

Air Fryer — Reheat Pre-Baked from Frozen

Place cooked frozen Yorkshires straight into basket at 180°C for 3–4 mins until crisp.

Conventional Oven — Muffin Tin

Preheat tin + oil at 220°C (200°C Fan) for 10 mins, pour batter, bake 20–25 mins without opening door.
