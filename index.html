💨 Air Fryer (Ramekins — Fresh or Fully Thawed)

Preheat 1 tsp oil at 200°C (390°F) for 4–5 mins until smoking hot. Whisk batter, pour in, and bake at 200°C for 10–12 mins without opening.

🔥 Fan Oven (12-Hole Muffin Tin)

Preheat ½ tsp oil per hole at 210°C Fan (425°F) for 10 mins. Pour batter in quickly and bake at 210°C Fan for 18–20 mins until deep golden.

❄️ Reheating Pre-Baked Frozen Puddings

Place straight from frozen into the air fryer at 180°C (355°F) for 3–4 mins until crisp and piping hot.
